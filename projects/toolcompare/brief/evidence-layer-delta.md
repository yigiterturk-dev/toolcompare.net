# Delta brief — evidence layer

Turn ToolCompare from a broad software directory into a source-backed decision
system without changing the approved public brand, routes or visual direction.

## Affected surfaces

- Tool records: source URLs, verification date, evidence status, fit and skip cases.
- Tool and comparison pages: visible provenance and claim boundaries.
- Quality gates: deterministic checks for sourced records and unsupported superlatives.
- First proof case: ChatGPT versus Claude.

## Locked

- Preserve the existing brand, routes, catalog and pricing-state semantics.
- Do not imply hands-on testing where only vendor documentation was reviewed.
- Do not deploy until build, content QA and visual QA pass.

## Unaffected

- Homepage art direction, typography, palette and navigation.
- Existing catalog entries that have not yet entered the evidence migration.
